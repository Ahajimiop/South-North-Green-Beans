# Design Plan: Personal Portfolio (ICT2215 Assessment 2)

## Target audience
Employers and lecturers who want to see a student's skills quickly.

## Wireframe (homepage)
```
+--------------------------------------+
| Zhang HaoTian        Home About Work Contact |
+--------------------------------------+
| Big headline + intro      | [ photo ] |
| [See my work]             |           |
+--------------------------------------+
| About: 3 cards (icon + title + text) |
+--------------------------------------+
| Work: 3 project cards + video        |
+--------------------------------------+
| Contact form                         |
+--------------------------------------+
| Footer                               |
+--------------------------------------+
```

## Colour palette
Ink #1F2A44 | Paper #F7F7F5 | Sand #E9E6DC | Teal #0F7C7A | Gold #F2B705

## Typography
Headings: Space Grotesk (bold). Body: Source Sans 3. Fallback: Arial.

## CRAP
Contrast: dark ink text on gold buttons, and light navigation text on a dark header.
Repetition: consistent cards, spacing, colours and fonts.
Alignment: main content follows a centred grid with left-aligned text; the footer text is centred.
Proximity: related icons, headings and descriptions are grouped inside each card.
